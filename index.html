How I think

My operating system.

Not a secret formula. A practical way of moving from a messy question to a useful next step.

Module 01

Research before assumptions

I investigate the market, audience, competitors and available evidence before recommending a direction.

notes → language map → hypothesis board
Module 02

AI as a thinking accelerator

I use tools such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Copilot to support research and drafting, then review the output for relevance, accuracy, voice and usefulness.

prompt → draft → review → final
Module 03

Ideas with a next step

I turn open-ended tasks into priorities, deliverables, timelines and follow-ups so the work can move forward.

task → priority → owner → follow-up
Module 04

Test instead of pretending to know

I distinguish evidence from assumptions and define what I would measure to learn whether an idea works.

hypothesis → experiment → observation → decision

Inside a workflow

Illustrative example, not a client deliverable.

01 Research
Start from public material (a founder talk, a post, a customer review) and note recurring themes, opinions and customer problems.
02 AI-assisted draft
Use an AI tool to cluster the notes and draft a first angle list. Fast, but treated as raw material.
03 Human review
Check every claim against the source, restore the founder’s voice, cut generic lines, and decide what is actually worth publishing.
